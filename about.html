Practical skills and innovation at St. Kizito High School Namugongo

Our school sits beside the historic Namugongo Catholic Shrine. Our students learn by building: cybersecurity defense, solar-powered robotics and AI that speaks their own languages.

Our story

Our students construct agricultural excavators and solar power setups from repurposed materials such as car wiper motors and discarded metal scrap. Nothing is too humble to become a working machine.

That habit of making things carries into our Cyber Team, where students learn to defend systems, solve capture the flag challenges, and program the microcontrollers and AI tools behind their own inventions.

Three areas we focus on

terminal

Cybersecurity

Ethical hacking and cryptography, practised through our own CTF challenges.

memory

Robotics and green energy

ESP32 microcontrollers drive our automated agricultural robots and sensors, powered by solar.

mic

Language-aware AI

Mukwano is a voice-controlled assistant that supports English and Luganda.

Our technology partner

Pearl Tech Hub, Naguru, Kampala

Pearl Tech Hub was founded in 2025 to close the technology skills gap in Africa. It trains professionals and builds software, cloud, networking and AI solutions, and it lists St. Kizito High School Namugongo among its partner schools.

Together we run STEM exhibitions and training, and we share projects such as the fire-fighting robot and the Bluetooth robotic arm. Pearl Tech Hub's Robotics Explorer lets visitors view these robots in 3D and ask its AI guide, Pearl, how they work.
